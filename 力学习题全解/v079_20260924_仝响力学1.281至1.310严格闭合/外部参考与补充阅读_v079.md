   https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-4-pendulums
2. Simple Harmonic Motion：周期、角频率、相位和平衡位置。
   https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-1-simple-harmonic-motion
3. Energy in Simple Harmonic Motion：`E=(1/2)kx^2+(1/2)mv^2=(1/2)kA^2`。
   https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-2-energy-in-simple-harmonic-motion
