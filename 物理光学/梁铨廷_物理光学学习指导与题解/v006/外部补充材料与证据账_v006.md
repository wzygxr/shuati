# v006 外部补充材料与证据账

外部资料只用于交叉核对模型、适用条件和现代应用，不替代梁铨廷教材题面，也不计入正式题数。

1. MIT OpenCourseWare, **Fraunhofer Diffraction - Two Slits**：双缝、有限缝宽包络与夫琅禾费实验图样。
   - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-006-video-demonstrations-in-lasers-and-optics-spring-2008/resources/fraunhofer-diffraction-2014-two-slits/
2. MIT OpenCourseWare, **Fraunhofer Diffraction - Multiple Slits**：多缝主极大、峰宽收缩与光栅行为。
   - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-006-video-demonstrations-in-lasers-and-optics-spring-2008/resources/fraunhofer-diffraction-2014-multiple-slits/
3. MIT OpenCourseWare, **Fresnel Diffraction - Adjustable Slit**：菲涅耳到夫琅禾费区的连续过渡。
   - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-006-video-demonstrations-in-lasers-and-optics-spring-2008/resources/fresnel-diffraction-2014-adjustable-slit/
4. MIT OpenCourseWare, **Modern Optics Project Laboratory**：Fraunhofer/Fresnel衍射、全息、成像与空间滤波。
   - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-161-modern-optics-project-laboratory-fall-2005/
5. NIST, **On the Diffraction Limit for Lensless Imaging**：Fraunhofer-Airy轮廓与分辨判据边界。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/diffraction-limit-lensless-imaging
6. NIST, **A Toolbox for Isophase-Curvature Guided Computation of Metrology Holograms**：计算全息和等相位曲线的精密计量应用。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/toolbox-isophase-curvature-guided-computation-metrology-holograms
7. NIST, **A Focal Plane Imager with High Dynamic Range to Identify Fabrication Errors in Diffractive Optics**：光栅和全息器件制造误差的焦平面表征。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/focal-plane-imager-high-dynamic-range-identify-fabrication-errors-diffractive-optics

证据等级：教材完整题号、上下文和书后答案承担原题证据；MIT与NIST资料只承担概念、实验和工程边界的交叉核对。
