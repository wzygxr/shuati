# v005 外部补充材料与证据账

外部资料只用于核对公式背景、现代术语、工程实现和测量边界，不替代梁铨廷教材原题，也不计入正式题数。

1. MIT OpenCourseWare, *Photonic Materials and Devices*, system-design lecture：DWDM、光纤、色散补偿与Fabry--Pérot干涉仪。
   - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-46-photonic-materials-and-devices-spring-2006/resources/3_46l3_sysdesign/
2. MIT OpenCourseWare, waveguide lecture：阶跃光纤、导模、V参数、模式数、群速度和单模条件。
   - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-46-photonic-materials-and-devices-spring-2006/resources/3_46l5_waveguide/
3. NIST, *Fabry-Perot Metrology for Displacements up to 50 mm*：自由光谱范围编码腔长和精密位移测量。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/fabry-perot-metrology-displacements-50-mm-1
4. NIST, *Robust Optical Design of Angled Multilayer Dielectric Mirrors*：多层四分之一波Bragg反射镜、带宽和膜厚梯度鲁棒性。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/robust-optical-design-angled-multilayer-dielectric-mirrors-optimized-rubidium-vapor
5. NIST, *Design and Demonstration of PECVD Angled Multilayer Dielectric Mirrors*：高反膜设计、膜厚变化与偏振保持。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/design-and-demonstration-pecvd-angled-multilayer-dielectric-mirrors-optimized
6. NIST, *Frequency stability of the mode spectrum of broad bandwidth Fabry-Pérot interferometers*：宽带FP谐振模用于频率校准及跨波长漂移。
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/frequency-stability-mode-spectrum-broad-bandwidth-fabry-perot-interferometers

证据等级：教材原题、正文和书后答案承担题号证据；MIT/NIST资料仅承担概念与应用补充。
